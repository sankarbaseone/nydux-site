The Problem

GPU clusters fail at the
systems layer. Not the hardware layer.

Hardware vendors sell you H100s. Network vendors sell you InfiniBand. Nobody designs the system between them. That gap is where $300K–$600K disappears every year.

01 // TOPOLOGY MISMATCH
NCCL ring fights your physical fabric
Your logical AllReduce topology doesn't match your switch fabric. Every training step pays a latency tax that compounds at scale.
02 // MTU FRAGMENTATION
Packets fragment at 4,096-byte boundary
Default MTU settings cause fragmentation on InfiniBand and RoCE networks. A single misconfiguration = 7× latency spike across the entire cluster.
03 // PARALLELISM MISMATCH
DP/TP/PP ratios wrong for your hardware
The wrong parallelism strategy for your cluster topology can halve your effective throughput. This decision is made at design time — not runtime.
04 // NO OBSERVABILITY
Dashboard shows healthy. Cluster is dying.
DCGM utilization shows 85%. Actual MFU is 7–12%. The gap is invisible without step-time instrumentation built into the architecture from day one.
What We Design

Fabric Architecture
